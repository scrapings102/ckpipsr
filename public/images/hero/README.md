# Hero Section Images

This folder contains local high-quality images used for the Hero banner slider section of the CKPIPSR portal.

## Recommended Image Guidelines
- **Aspect Ratio**: `16:9` (e.g., 1920x1080 or 2400x1350 pixels)
- **Format**: `.webp` or `.jpg` or `.png`
- **File Sizes**: Keep under 500KB for faster loading speeds

## Image Assets Included:
- `pharmacy_lab.png` - Modern pharmaceutical science and research laboratory
- `college_campus.png` - Academic building and campus gardens
- `students_learning.png` - Students collaborating on pharmaceutical research
